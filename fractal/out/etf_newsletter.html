HOW TO READ THIS
RISK RANGE
The high and low price is likely to trade between today, from recent volatility. It is an envelope, not a direction call — it says how far, not which way. The edges are where decisions get made: at the low end you are being offered the best price of the session, at the high end the worst. Levels are fixed by yesterday’s close and hold all day, so price moves through them rather than with them.
TRADE
The short-duration line. Price above it is bullish TRADE, below is bearish. It is the tactical read — where to add inside a position you already hold, and the first thing to break when momentum turns.
TREND
The cycle line, and the one that matters. Price above it is bullish TREND. A break of TREND is a regime change, not a wobble; a break of TRADE while TREND holds is usually noise inside an intact move.
PUTTING THEM TOGETHER
Both bullish is trending long; both bearish is trending short; one of each is counter-trend. The rule that saves the most money: do not buy the low end of the RANGE while TRADE is broken. Wait for TREND to hold. Those names sit under WATCHLIST rather than ADD LONG.
VOLUME
How unusual today’s volume is for that name, measured in standard deviations rather than percent — a +60% day is routine for a thin fund and remarkable for a large one. Beyond ±2 is flagged. It does not generate a signal on its own; it tells you whether a move happened on conviction or on nobody trading.
